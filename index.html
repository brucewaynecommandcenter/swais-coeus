The name

Named for the questioning mind.

In Greek mythology, Coeus is the Titan of intellect and inquiry. We chose the name for the international chapter of SWAIS, Saraf Worldsphere AI Services, because that is what good education does: it teaches people to ask better questions and find their own answers.

SWAIS COEUS brings a proven AI learning and operations platform, built and tested in some of the most demanding school environments in India, to schools, institutions and enterprises across Europe.

Our heritage

A young company with seven decades behind it.

SWAIS is backed by the Saraf family, whose seven decades of industrial, philanthropic, national and international work span mining, metals and energy, community service and honorary diplomatic posts.

After five decades in ferro alloys manufacturing and other industries, Mr. Ashish Saraf took up the mantra of AI and IT, finding it the most fitting way to do a greater service to society. SWAIS is that commitment in action.

The same Garividi region where the family's township has run schools and a hospital for decades is home to the SWAIS development centre today.
